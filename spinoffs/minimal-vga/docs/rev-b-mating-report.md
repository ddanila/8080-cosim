## VGA cable access

Video is in the end slot, with slot 4 empty. The checked exact VGA footprint places the connector mating face **5.80 mm beyond the Video top PCB edge**, so a straight VGA plug exits away from the card stack. This is footprint/datasheet evidence (`rev-b-video-parts.md`), not a STEP-body claim.

## Reversed-card keying

The correct extension row is at X=14.45; a 180-degree reversed card moves it to X=85.55, where no socket exists. The base row is centred and symmetric.

Keying is therefore **convention-only**: matching pin-1/orientation silk is mandatory and insertion/removal must occur with power off. The extension row does not prove a hard mechanical block against partial reversed insertion.

## Result

**PASS** — all four populated card envelopes clear at the frozen slot assignment; end-slot VGA cable access and the residual convention-only orientation risk are explicitly recorded.
