T31HOST.BIN - JUKURAVI LOW-4K HOST MONITOR
===========================================

Image size: 8192 bytes
ROM version: 1Ah
Self CRC16: 72EF
SHA256: a4fed9185616bbfbef22ab6f0b18202e6d79ad7dbe3b7c46a77a700d3af3676c

Program T31HOST.BIN at offset 0000h into an AT28C64B and verify it. Install
it in Juku D15 and leave D16 empty.

The real T30 image repeatedly sent its exact banner, status 08, and status 83,
then restarted when execution entered ROM address 100Ch. T31 keeps every
executed monitor byte below that suspect boundary; the loader ends at 0FFFh.

T31 enters the loader directly after compact RAM status 83. It bypasses the
unreachable full-C000h-CFFFh workspace sweep and loader-ROM checksum tail, so
the programmer's successful verify is the ROM-integrity evidence for this
workaround. Bounded TxRDY recovery and T28 commands/CALL/RET remain available.

Simulation reproduces T30's exact repeated reset cycle at 100Ch. Under the
same forced boundary plus the known D55 fault, T31 reaches READY, uploads and
verifies code, executes CALL/RET, and returns A plus a RAM result block.
