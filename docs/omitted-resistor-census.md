# `.009` passive omission census

Status: **OPEN PLACEMENT / ENDPOINT GAP**. This compares the exact-revision drawings and
owner board photos with the passive refs and placements in `kicad/juku.board.json`. A missing
number alone does not prove an omitted component: the `.009` board retires
the `.006` RF section, and some positions may be unpopulated. The rows below
separate source-proved omissions from candidates awaiting source disposition.

Exact sheet-1 supply detail `PXL_20260718_101827714.jpg` places the printed
groups `C9...C12`, `C15`, `C74...C78`, `C82...C93`, `C94...C98`, and `C100`
beside a +5 V-to-ground bypass symbol. The same detail explicitly draws C92
between ground and −12 V and C93 between ground and +12 V. Those individual
branches override any collective-annotation reading for C92/C93. This source
evidence does not establish each part's physical population, pad location,
value, or owner-board copper.
