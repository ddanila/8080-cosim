# CS00024 construction-variant observations

Status date: 2026-08-22

CS00024 differs physically from every other Juku machine inspected by the
owner so far. These are direct owner observations, not yet a factory revision
identification; no photographs, assembly designation, or manufacturing date
have been admitted as evidence for the variant.

## Observed differences

- The speaker is attached non-removably to the PSU assembly. In the other
  inspected machines it is a separate unit.
- The lower central area on the reverse of the keyboard PCB lacks the
  `децимальный номер` (decimal/document designation) present on the other
  inspected keyboard boards.
- The CS00024 PSU socket has an additional bracket. That bracket is
  mechanically incompatible with the CS00000 board connector.

These observations make CS00024 a distinct construction variant until proved
otherwise. They do not yet establish whether the PSU electronics, keyboard
circuit, or mainboard electrical interface differ. In particular, a PSU that
is electrically suitable must not be assumed to be a drop-in mechanical
replacement across machines.

## Useful follow-up evidence

- Photograph the complete PSU assembly, speaker attachment, connector and
  bracket from several angles, with any labels readable.
- Photograph both sides of the CS00024 keyboard PCB and the corresponding
  designation area on one comparison keyboard.
- Record PSU, keyboard and chassis assembly markings plus connector pin count,
  keying and wire colours before assigning a revision or interchangeability
  claim.
